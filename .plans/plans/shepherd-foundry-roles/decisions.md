| 16 | The sheepdog's default limits | Team idle: 60 minutes with nothing running, then the team's top agent is nudged once and its parent told; hourly after that until it moves or is snoozed. Every limit is an owner setting, and a role file may set its own. | Foundry `team-idle-watch.sh` (owner, 2026-10-23: "so I don't have to babysit the workflow"; `IDLE_SECONDS` 3600). `capacity-retry.sh` (owner, 2026-11-01: "the request is retried after a 5 min backoff immediately without needing a model to see and decide"; `MAX_RETRIES` 12).
