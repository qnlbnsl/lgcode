| 10 | What is a channel? | Planner's reading. A channel is a named, ordered list of posts with a member list. It has no team, role or phase in it; the roles plan decides which channels exist. | 11 | Who may open a channel and change its members? | Planner's reading. The owner may do anything. An agent may open a channel and becomes its manager. A manager may add only agents whose nearest available ancestor is the manager (the engine's own rule for `prompt`, `holderAbove`) and the manager's own nearest available ancestor. Siblings and cousins are joined by their common ancestor or the owner. A manager may remove any member. If a manager stops or crashes, its rights pass to its nearest available ancestor, as the engine's parent rights do. | Keeps a channel inside the authority its creator already has, so the board gives no agent a reach the engine denies it. | 12 | Who may send a direct message to whom? | Planner's reading. | 13 | Who can read a channel? | Planner's reading. Its members and the owner, through `board read`. | 14 | What does a post carry? | Planner's reading. No kind label in this plan: the wake rule needs only `needsReply`. | 15 | Who is woken by a post? | A post that needs a reply wakes exactly its addressees, minus its author. Every other post wakes nobody. The owner's posts follow the same rule. | Epic "Done when" (owner's epic, 2026-10-30): "A message on the board wakes only the agent that needs to answer it." `docs/features.md`, "Wake rules": "Only a post that needs an answer wakes its recipient, because every wake costs a full agent turn." | 16 | What closes a post that needs a reply, and who hears the answer? | Planner's reading. A reply to it from one of its addressees, or from the owner, closes it. A reply from anyone else is an ordinary post and closes nothing. | Without this the asker would have to poll for its answer, which the engine's design forbids ("Nothing polls"). | 17 | How are several wakes for one agent combined? | Planner's reading. An agent has at most one board wake waiting to be sent. | 18 | When does an agent see posts that did not wake it? | Planner's reading.
